Login page options

Three directions for the new sign-in screen, built from our brand colors, type and pill buttons. The logo is a placeholder wordmark until your brand files arrive. Try each one in light and dark, on desktop and phone, and with the error states.

A · Centered card

The safest and most familiar. A soft brand-tinted background with one clear card. Best if your logo is a compact mark or wordmark and you want the least visual noise.

B · Split screen

The most “modern product” feel. A brand panel with a tagline and three product points beside the form; it folds into a simple header on phones. Best if you have a strong logo and want a short product line.

C · Minimal

Quiet and fast: no card, the form sits on the page with a calm graphic beside it. Best if you want sign-in to feel light, and the logo carries the brand.

What to tell me: the letter you prefer (or a mix), and anything to change.
