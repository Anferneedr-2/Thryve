Workspace, redesigned: assistants first, chats below

This is idea 2 with the change you asked for. The left panel now starts with your assistants and shows your recent chats underneath. The context panel is hidden until you open it, so the conversation gets the room it needs. In the first mockup, click an assistant to filter the chats, use the panel button at the left of the chat header to hide the chats panel, and the one at the right to show or hide the details panel. Both side panels work the same way. The name and role block at the bottom of the panel is gone; your avatar lives in the rail, and the tokens left are in the context panel.

What is new

  • An Assistants section at the top of the left panel: one row per chatbot the person can use, with its description.
  • Choosing an assistant filters the recent chats and sets who the next new chat is with.
  • Recent chats show which assistant each belongs to.

What stays from idea 2

  • The slim icon rail for the whole app (Chat, Attendance, People, Agents, Settings).
  • A separate list column, so history never covers the chat.
  • The same brand colors, pill buttons and fonts.

Roomier than before

  • Context panel closed by default: the chat column is about 850 px instead of 530 px.
  • 17 px text, 34 px between messages, 112 px composer.
  • AI replies are plain text on the page, with no bubble and no avatar, across the whole column (up to 1,180 px). Headings, tables, lists and code get the full width. Only your own message sits in a bubble.
  • Rows are 64 px or taller, with 16 px padding.
  • Each side panel hides completely with one button, for the most room when you want it.

Three small decisions for you

  1. Clicking an assistant: in this design it filters the chats and sets who the next new chat is with. Alternative: it immediately opens a new chat with that assistant. Which feels right?
  2. The context panel: closed by default (as shown), opened with the panel button. Alternative: open by default on wide screens (1,500 px and up). Closed keeps the chat roomy.
  3. People with only one assistant: show the Assistants section anyway (one row, consistent), or hide it and give the chats the full height? I lean towards hiding it, since there is nothing to choose.

Which assistants someone sees still follows the access rules: General for everyone, and any others given by an admin. Admins and Super Admins see every enabled assistant.
