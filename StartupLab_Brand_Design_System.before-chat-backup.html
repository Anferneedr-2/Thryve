Innovation Hub · Cavite · Light + dark theme

One confident blue for every person, startup and business in the ecosystem.

This system styles the StartupLab brand as seen on startuplab.ph: bright white sections, a single electric blue, deep navy headlines, soft pill buttons and friendly rounded cards. Each audience (students, startups, businesses, mentors, community, AI and tech) gets its own small accent color, used only on icons and tags.

Rules

  • On navy or blue, place the mark on a white rounded tile (radius 12) and set the wordmark in white.

02. Voice and tone

StartupLab sounds like a capable, welcoming community. Clear, practical and encouraging; never jargon-heavy or hypey. Headlines are short and rhythmic, often using three beats.

Practical

Say what people get and what to do next.

SayLearn. Build. Launch.

Welcoming

Speak to students, founders and owners as equals.

SayYour path to growth starts here.

Grounded

Real support, real places, real people. Avoid empty superlatives.

AvoidRevolutionary, game-changing, disruptive.
Headline patterns seen on startuplab.ph
  • “The Innovation Hub Where People, Startups, and Businesses Grow Together.”
  • “One ecosystem. Your path to growth.” (short statement, then a promise)
  • “Learn. Build. Launch Your Career Inside a Real Ecosystem.” (verbs in sequence)
  • Button labels start with a verb: “Explore the Ecosystem”, “Get in Touch”.

06. Motion

The site feels premium because motion is slow to start and gentle to land. One easing curve is used everywhere. Everything stops when the device asks for reduced motion.

Fast · 200ms

Hover color, focus ring, button press.

--dur-fast

Standard · 380ms

Card lift, menu open, fade-up on scroll.

--dur-standard

Large · 700ms

Section reveals, larger panels.

--dur-large

Hero · 1400ms

Ecosystem orbit and hero entrance only.

--dur-hero
Easing

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) as --ease-premium. Cards lift 4px with a shadow and blue border on hover; buttons rise 1px and shrink to 98% on press.

15. Usage principles

Keep every page bright, calm and obvious about the next step.

Do

  • Use one blue. Let white space, navy headlines and pale blue fills do the rest.
  • Keep one primary pill button per view.
  • Use audience accents on icons and small tags only.
  • Write headlines in short beats and button labels that start with a verb.
  • Alternate white and canvas sections; end with one navy band.

Avoid

  • Gradients on buttons, or more than one gradient glow per section.
  • Accent colors for body text or large backgrounds.
  • Square buttons or sharp-cornered cards.
  • Light grey text under 4.5:1 contrast.
  • Heavy shadows or decoration that competes with the content.

Accessibility checklist

  • Blue #315EEF on white is about 5.3:1 and white on blue is the same, above the 4.5:1 minimum.
  • Every icon-only control has an aria-label. Inputs have labels; errors use aria-describedby and aria-invalid.
  • Focus is always visible: a 2px blue outline with a 2px offset.
  • Tap targets are 36px or larger (44px for main buttons).
  • Animations are removed when the device asks for reduced motion.
  • Light is the default. Dark follows the device setting until the visitor picks a theme, and the choice is remembered (section 16).

Rules

  • Primary buttons keep the exact same #315EEF fill with white text in both themes (5.3:1).
  • The navy dark button flips to a pale button in dark so it still stands out.
  • The flask logo is navy, so in dark it always sits on a white rounded tile.
  • Shadows get heavier and black; borders carry more of the separation.
  • Swatches in this document always show the true light-theme brand colors.

Implementation

  • Set data-theme="dark" on the html element and override the tokens below.
  • Initial theme: saved choice, else prefers-color-scheme. Set it in the head before paint to avoid a flash.
  • Use --brand for fills and --blue-600 for blue text, never the other way round.
  • Set color-scheme: dark so native controls match.
